# Business and side tracks

## Why it matters

A Plone conference draws mostly people who already build with Plone.
A side track reaches a different audience in the same week, for example decision-makers who need no prior Plone knowledge, a national users day or a co-located conference.
It can also carry its conversations into the sprint, as the 2026 Business Track did (S082).

It also adds risk.
A planned morning was cancelled once because of school holidays and a late announcement, and a business-matching round was cancelled once because the partner who was to run it did not take part.
A side track competes for the same rooms, volunteers and speakers as the main program.

The requirements register lists no side-track requirement as of 2026-11, so the choice is yours.
This chapter helps you decide whether to add a side track, and it gives the 2026 Business Track as a worked example of how one was built.
How a side-track ticket is priced and sold is covered in `ticketing-and-registration`.

## Applies when

- `all` Any conference can add a side track, and the chapter helps a team decide whether to.

## Decisions you must make

- {bdg-secondary}`OPTION` **Do you add a side track, and for which audience?** A track for decision-makers, a users day or a co-located conference each needs its own promise and its own marketing.
  Write the promise in one sentence before you commit.
  (decide-by: T-12m)
- {bdg-primary}`DEFAULT` (basis: L-2038, L-3103) **Who must commit before you announce a component, and by which date?** One conference lost a planned morning to a late announcement, and another lost a round when its named partner did not take part.
  Get written commitment from any partner who runs part of the track and announce early.
  (decide-by: T-9m)
- {bdg-secondary}`OPTION` **Does the side track run in parallel with the talks, or on a separate day?** Conferences have run a standalone day, a day before the main conference, a full parallel day and a co-located conference that took one day of the talk program.
  Parallel use costs room and volunteer capacity on the main days.
  (decide-by: T-9m)
- {bdg-secondary}`OPTION` **Is the side track part of the conference pass, or sold on its own?** A separate ticket, a one-day ticket that includes it and a party-only ticket are all possible.
  Decide with `ticketing-and-registration`.
  (decide-by: T-8m)
- {bdg-secondary}`OPTION` **Do you use an early local event as a live test?** One conference ran a users day before the main event and used it to test the Wi-Fi, room equipment and wayfinding.
  (decide-by: T-6m)

## How-to

### Define the track

1. Write the audience and the promise in one sentence, for example decision-makers and no prior Plone knowledge required.
   Done looks like a sentence you can put on the website.
1. Choose the format and day, using the decisions above.
   Done looks like a slot on the draft schedule that does not clash with school holidays or a travel-heavy week.
1. List every partner whose participation the track depends on and get their commitment in writing.
   Done looks like a written yes from each, or the component removed.

### Build the arc

1. {bdg-secondary}`OPTION` Give each slot one claim, so the day tells one story instead of presenting unrelated talks.
   Done looks like a one-line claim per slot on the program page.
1. {bdg-secondary}`OPTION` Order the day by dramaturgy, not by who said yes first.
   The 2026 day was a full-day track with one invited keynote and seven practitioners (S082).
   Done looks like a running order that you can defend slot by slot.
1. {bdg-secondary}`OPTION` Include a slot that adds caution, so the day stays credible.
   Done looks like at least one speaker whose message tempers the others.
1. Publish the slot length with the program.
   Done looks like one length per slot on the program page.

### Run and follow up

1. {bdg-secondary}`OPTION` Host the day with one person who knows the arc and opens and closes it.
   Done looks like a moderation script that names the arc.
1. Carry the conversation into the sprint if you have one (S082).
   Done looks like an invitation at the closing conversation to continue in the sprint room.
1. Record attendance and the source of each ticket.
   Done looks like numbers a later team can compare.


```{warning} Side track announced too late or on a clashing week
:class: hb-risk hb-likelihood-medium hb-impact-medium
risk | likelihood: medium | impact: medium | mitigation: Fix the format and day when you plan the program and announce them with the first public program notice | owner-role: marketing-lead

One conference lost a morning to school holidays, travel fatigue and a late announcement.
```

```{warning} A side-track speaker withdraws
:class: hb-risk hb-likelihood-medium hb-impact-medium
risk | likelihood: medium | impact: medium | mitigation: Keep a replacement rule for each slot, because one withdrawal breaks a day built as a single arc | owner-role: program-lead | playbook: speaker-dropout

Each slot carries one claim in the arc, so a gap in the running order shows.
```
