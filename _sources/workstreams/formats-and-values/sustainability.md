# Sustainability

## Why it matters

A conference moves many people by air and train, fills bins and prints paper.
The Plone Foundation board has adopted two priorities, more diversity in the community and a lower carbon footprint, and asks organizers to say when meeting them needs extra funding (L-4050; S005, as of 2026-11).

Past teams made pledges and small gestures, but no archived account measures a footprint or reports what worked.
This chapter is therefore a short list of practical measures and an open invitation to contribute evidence.

## Applies when

- `all` Every conference uses energy, creates waste and asks people to travel.

## How-to

1. Ask the venue and the caterer what they recycle and whether they can avoid disposables.
   Done: a written answer for each.
2. Keep printed handouts minimal and choose swag for quality, as described in [Print and merch](../on-site-production/print-and-merch.md) (L-4071; S049).
   Done: the print and swag list shows what was left out.
3. Prefer locally produced food in the catering brief (L-4071; S049).
   Done: the brief names the preference.
4. Decide on remote access, using [Hybrid and online](hybrid-online.md).
   Done: a recorded decision.
5. Plan what happens to equipment after the event, for example a donation (L-2034; S016).
   Done: a list with a receiver per item.
6. Tell the Foundation if a goal needs extra funding (L-4050; S005).
   Done: a dated message to the board.
