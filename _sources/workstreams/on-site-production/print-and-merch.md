# Print and merch

## Why it matters

Printed things look small on a budget line and cause large problems on the first morning.
A wrong shirt count, a badge with a typo or a schedule that ran out each show up in front of every attendee at once.

Most items depend on a number that is not known until late, the attendee count.
Suppliers need that number early, registrations keep arriving, and the team ends up reordering or reprinting under time pressure.
Late buyers also expect to get a shirt.

Print is also where the team's values show, in the quality of the shirts and in how much paper and swag ends up in the bin.
For that side, see [Sustainability](../formats-and-values/sustainability.md).
Badge handout and signs are covered in [Signage and showtime](signage-and-showtime.md).

## Applies when

- `all` Every conference prints at least badges and a schedule.

## Decisions you must make

- {bdg-primary}`DEFAULT` (basis: L-1140, L-3040) **Will you print a schedule even though it is online?**
  Printed schedules were popular at one conference and judged worth the money at another.
  (decide-by: T-3m)
- {bdg-primary}`DEFAULT` (basis: L-1136, L-4046) **How many shirts above the attendee count do you order?**
  One team advised at least 10% more than attendees (as of 2006, S009).
  Late buyers take the extras, and leftovers can be sold after the conference.
  (decide-by: T-3m)
- {bdg-primary}`DEFAULT` (basis: L-1142, L-2086, L-3052) **Who prints badges, with how many blanks, and on which printer?**
  Print extra blank badges and keep a printer and paper on site.
  (decide-by: T-3m)
- {bdg-secondary}`OPTION` **Which shirt do you order, and how do you collect sizes?**
  One team paid extra for ethically made shirts in two cuts (L-1135) and collected sizes in a separate survey that worked, though registration data would be more exact (L-1137).
  An attendee asked for pre-shrunk shirts (L-1144).
  (decide-by: T-4m)
- {bdg-secondary}`OPTION` **Is the print shop local, and do you order in batches?**
  Carrying shirts in from far away was a burden (L-1138).
  Another team ordered in two batches against registrations (L-2007).
  (decide-by: T-4m)
- {bdg-secondary}`OPTION` **What goes in the attendee bag?**
  Contents ranged from a shirt, tote and sponsor-donated credit (L-3132) to goodies confirmed only after 200 registrations (L-2085).
  (decide-by: T-3m)
- {bdg-secondary}`OPTION` **Do you hang banners in the rooms?**
  One conference used vinyl banners as video backdrop and branding, then reused them (L-1143).
  (decide-by: T-3m)

## How-to

### Months before
1. Fix the shirt design and quantity plan, with sizes collected at registration if the form allows.
   Done: a written size plan.
2. Brief every supplier early, and ask each to confirm sizes and quantities in writing.
   Done: a signed-off supplier list.
3. Order large wall schedules well in advance, because they are slow and expensive (L-3053; S029).
   Done: an order date on the production calendar.
4. Plan printed material to include maps of parties, sponsored hotels, places to eat and main transport routes (L-3055; S029).
   Done: a map sheet in the brochure draft.

### Late in the run-up
5. Order shirts after the August program work and prepare the attendee bag in September, as the Foundation's planning schedule places them (L-4043; S004, as of 2026-11).
   Done: the order is placed with counts taken from the registration numbers.
6. Reconfirm every item, size and quantity with each supplier two weeks before the event.
   Done: a written reply per supplier.
7. Print badges, with extra blanks, and sort them as the signage chapter describes.
   Done: a labeled box per sort order.

### On site
8. Keep a printer, paper and stickers at the registration desk.
   Done: one test page printed before doors open.

## Risks

```{warning} Suppliers deliver wrong or late items in the final week
:class: hb-risk hb-likelihood-medium hb-impact-medium
risk | likelihood: medium | impact: medium | mitigation: Brief suppliers months ahead, then reconfirm every item, size and quantity in writing two weeks before the event | owner-role: merch lead

Judged for a typical mid-size conference; recovery costs many phone calls.
```

```{warning} Late registrations outrun the print buffer
:class: hb-risk hb-likelihood-medium hb-impact-low
risk | likelihood: medium | impact: low | mitigation: Order a buffer above the registration count, keep blank badges and bring a printer to the venue | owner-role: registration lead

Second print runs and last-minute bag filling were recorded at two conferences.
```
