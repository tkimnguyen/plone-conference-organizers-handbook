# Playbook: speaker dropout

Open this playbook when a scheduled speaker, trainer or keynote cannot give their session.
It covers the first hour; program and speaker care are in `scheduling`, `speakers-and-speaker-care` and `trainings`.

## Triggers

- A speaker withdraws before the conference, by message or through a colleague.
- A keynote speaker withdraws, which leaves a plenary slot and a published headline empty.
- A trainer or speaker does not appear at the start of their session.
- A speaker's visa is refused or their travel is canceled.
- A speaker stops answering messages in the final week before the conference.

## First-hour actions

1. Confirm the withdrawal in writing, and ask whether a co-speaker or a colleague can give the session in their place.
2. Check the wait list from the call for proposals and offer the slot to the first suitable session.
3. For a training that has started without its trainer, ask experienced community members in the room or nearby to cover it, and tell the participants what they will get instead.
4. For a keynote, choose one replacement: promote a strong talk to the plenary, form a short panel, or invite a community keynote.
5. If nothing fits, turn the slot into lightning talks or an open space, and announce it as such.
6. Update every surface from one place: website schedule, app, room signs and the information desk.
   Done looks like one person confirming that all surfaces show the same slot.
7. If paid training seats are affected, tell the registration lead which participants need a refund, a transfer or an alternative.
8. Record the gap and the replacement for the post-mortem, without naming the speaker.

## Owner roles

| Role | Responsibility | Full advice in |
|---|---|---|
| Program lead | Chooses the replacement and owns the schedule change. | `scheduling` |
| Speaker care lead | Contacts the withdrawing speaker and the replacement, and handles travel and accommodation changes. | `speakers-and-speaker-care` |
| Training coordinator | Finds cover for a training and briefs its participants. | `trainings` |
| Communications lead | Publishes the change on every surface at once. | `web-comms-brand` |
| Registration lead | Handles refunds or transfers for affected paid sessions. | `ticketing-and-registration` |

### What history says

- In 2006 four sessions were wait-listed in case a speaker canceled; none did, but the list was ready (L-1069 in `cfp-and-selection#case-notes`).
  Having a second person who knew every session and presenter made the agenda work much easier (L-1077).
- In 2010 several speakers withdrew as late as two weeks before the event, which narrowed the range of talks (L-2011 in `speakers-and-speaker-care#case-notes`).
- In 2011 enough scheduled speakers canceled that late cancellation is recorded as a recurring risk (L-3069).
- In 2023 trainings went well except for one missing presenter, whose session experienced community members covered (L-4186 in `trainings#case-notes`).
- A State of Plone keynote is a community tradition, with no tradition about who gives it; a replacement for that slot needs the same care (L-3066).
