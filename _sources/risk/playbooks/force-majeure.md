# Playbook: force majeure

Open this playbook when an event outside anyone's control threatens the conference as a whole.
It covers the first hour; contracts, insurance and the online format are in `contracts-legal-insurance` and `hybrid-online`.

## Triggers

- The authorities restrict gatherings or travel in the host city or region, or in the countries most attendees come from.
- A natural disaster, a public-health emergency, a strike or a transport shutdown affects the dates.
- A security threat or a civil emergency affects the venue or the city.
- A large share of speakers or attendees cannot travel, for example after a change in entry rules.
- A long power or network outage takes out the venue during the conference.

## First-hour actions

1. If people are on site, follow the instructions of the authorities and the venue, and account for every team member and volunteer.
   The safety response is in `health-safety-emergencies`.
2. Establish the facts from official sources and write them down with the date and time of each.
3. Convene the decision group: conference chair, treasurer, Foundation liaison and the venue liaison.
4. Name the options and the date by which each must be chosen: proceed, proceed with changes, postpone, move online, or cancel.
5. Read the force-majeure clauses of the venue, catering and hotel contracts, and the insurance policy if one exists.
6. Freeze every spending decision that can still wait.
7. Publish one holding statement with the time of the next update, and tell speakers and sponsors directly.
8. Start a record of costs, decisions and communications, because any claim or refund later depends on it.

## Owner roles

| Role | Responsibility | Full advice in |
|---|---|---|
| Conference chair | Leads the decision group and approves every public statement. | `team-and-governance` |
| Safety contact | Leads the on-site safety response and liaises with emergency services. | `health-safety-emergencies` |
| Treasurer | Reads the contracts and the policy, freezes spending and keeps the cost record. | `contracts-legal-insurance` |
| Foundation liaison | Brings the Foundation into the decision, especially where it is financial agent. | `legal-entity-and-financial-agent` |
| Online lead | Prepares the online option and its platform. | `hybrid-online` |
| Communications lead | Publishes statements and keeps attendees, speakers and sponsors informed. | `web-comms-brand` |

### What history says

- In 2020 the Foundation board canceled the physical conference because of the pandemic and ran it entirely online (L-4163 in `hybrid-online#case-notes`).
  The online edition kept the week but moved training to a weekend, and took place three weeks later than first announced (L-4164, L-4165).
  Registration ran until the day before the start, and the call for papers stayed open until about six weeks before (L-4166, L-4167).
- In 2021 the conference was again fully online, on a platform provided by a sponsoring company, and the Foundation took over organizing as financial agent (L-4168, L-4170).
- Organizers of the online editions found that no platform recreated chance encounters in the hallway (L-4180).
  Remote attendees miss real-time questions in training, hallway conversations and the sprint (L-4178).
- In 2026 the venue-insurance due diligence was never completed (L-5034).
