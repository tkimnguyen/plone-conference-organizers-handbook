# Playbook: venue loss

Open this playbook when the contracted venue cancels or can no longer host the conference as agreed.
It covers the first hour; venue choice and contracts are in `venue` and `contracts-legal-insurance`.

## Triggers

- The venue cancels the booking or announces that the dates are no longer available.
- The venue becomes unusable through damage, a power or safety failure, or an order from the authorities.
- The venue double-books rooms or withdraws rooms, catering or network service that the contract includes.
- The venue changes terms so that the conference can no longer run as planned, for example by blocking network use.
- Restrictions affect every venue in the city, not only yours; open [Playbook: force majeure](force-majeure.md) instead.

## First-hour actions

1. Get the cancellation or change in writing, with the reason and the date it takes effect.
   Do not sign or agree anything in the first conversation.
2. Read the contract's cancellation, relocation and force-majeure clauses, and write down the refund and compensation each one gives.
3. Inform the conference chair, the treasurer and the Foundation liaison, and the insurer if the conference holds a policy.
4. Freeze dependent commitments that are not yet binding: catering, AV, the hotel room block and print.
5. List the options with a deadline for each: another venue from the original shortlist, a split across several venues, a date shift, or an online format.
6. Ask the venue for its own relocation help and for the contacts of comparable venues.
7. Write one holding statement for attendees, speakers and sponsors: what happened, that the team is working on it, and when the next update comes.
   Speakers and sponsors hear it directly before it is public.
8. Set the date by which the team will decide, and put it in the statement.

## Owner roles

| Role | Responsibility | Full advice in |
|---|---|---|
| Conference chair | Leads the decision on the option and approves public statements. | `team-and-governance` |
| Venue liaison | Deals with the venue, gathers alternatives and checks their capacity. | `venue` |
| Treasurer | Reads the money side of the contract, freezes spending and talks to the insurer. | `contracts-legal-insurance` |
| Foundation liaison | Informs the Foundation and asks for its decision where it is the financial agent. | `legal-entity-and-financial-agent` |
| Communications lead | Publishes the holding statement and the decision. | `web-comms-brand` |

### What history says

- In 2010 only three venues in the host city had enough rooms and a main room for keynotes, which shows how short a replacement list can be (L-2002 in `city-selection#case-notes`).
- In 2012 the venue's network blocked non-work use, and the organizers solved it with a dedicated line rather than a move (L-2076).
- In 2020 the Foundation board canceled the physical conference because of the pandemic and ran it online (L-4163 in `hybrid-online#case-notes`).
  The online edition kept the week's shape and took place three weeks later than first announced (L-4164, L-4165).
- In 2026 the venue-insurance due diligence was never completed, so the team did not know what a policy would cover (L-5034 in `contracts-legal-insurance#case-notes`).
- The archive holds no account of a Plone conference that lost its venue and moved to another one; the steps above rest on contract practice, not on a recorded case.
