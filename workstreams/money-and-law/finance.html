Finance#

Why it matters#

A Plone conference spends money before it earns any. Deposits, catering and equipment fall due while sponsor and ticket income is still arriving, so the team carries a gap that grows toward the final weeks. Teams that did not plan for the gap fronted costs from private funds or borrowed from friends.

The Plone Foundation may act as financial agent and collects a share of registration income. The host company or association rarely recovers its staff time in full. VAT adds difficulty when the organizer and the event sit in different countries.

Good finance work means a budget the whole team can read, cash when bills arrive, and books a successor can pick up. The steps are in the How-to section.

Applies when#

  • budget The money flow differs by model: a seed loan, the Foundation as financial agent, or the organizers' own risk.

  • jurisdiction VAT, accounting rules and payment options depend on the country of the organizer and of the event.

Decisions you must make#

  • MUST (req: R-007; as-of: 2026-11-02) Does your proposal state whether the Foundation acts as financial agent? The Foundation's event financial policy requires an explicit statement. The legal side belongs to legal-entity-and-financial-agent. (decide-by: T-18m)

  • MUST (req: R-008; as-of: 2026-11-02) If the Foundation is your financial agent, have you budgeted your own work hours? The Foundation takes the financial risk and lends its bank accounts, accounting systems and Treasurer, but does not pay the organizers' extra hours. (decide-by: T-18m)

  • MUST (req: R-009; as-of: 2026-11-02) If the Foundation is your financial agent, have you agreed Board updates and Board authority? The Board receives regular updates, joins the planning, decides on all financial matters and receives any surplus. (decide-by: T-18m)

  • DEFAULT (basis: L-2020, L-4028, L-4149) Do you have a full budget at three attendance levels before you commit money? Include sponsor income, ticket income, all expenses and the Foundation line. Hosting calls from 2015 to 2019 required it (R-004, R-005), and no later statement confirms it. (decide-by: T-12m)

  • DEFAULT (basis: L-1031, L-3008, L-3096) How will you cover costs that fall due before income arrives? Name the bridge before signing: a seed loan, the financial agent, a reserve or a bank facility. Hosting calls required a plan (R-006), and no later statement confirms it. (decide-by: T-12m)

  • DEFAULT (basis: L-2023, L-4077, L-4900) What share of registration income goes to the Foundation, and is it in the budget? Calls from 2015 to 2019 set 15 percent of registration income, not of sponsorship, with one call at 20 percent (R-010). The rate for your year is a question for the Board (see Thin content). (decide-by: T-12m)

  • OPTION Which VAT regime applies to your ticket income, and who verified it? Settle this before you set prices (L-5000). One conference reports the evidence, so this stays an option. A qualified adviser should confirm place of supply and refund rules. (decide-by: T-12m)

  • OPTION Will you keep a plain-text budget and ledger from the first ticket sale? One team paired a budget with a shadow double-entry ledger (L-5014) and kept it from the start of sales (L-5044).

  • OPTION Will you apply for outside grants, and keep them out of committed income? An unanswered request cannot fund a contract (L-5006).

How-to#

  1. Build the budget from the Foundation's planning schedule: sponsor and ticket income against shirts, trainer housing, venue, services, catering, optional travel support and the Foundation share (L-4028). Done when the budget shows low, expected and high attendance.

  2. Open shared budget and accounting books when ticket sales begin (L-5044). Done when every commitment signed after that day is booked against the budget.

  3. Make repeated cumulative imports idempotent and reconcile balances after each cycle (L-5060). Done when a second import changes no balance.

  4. Track contracted but uninvoiced obligations in a report-only overlay, apart from statutory balances and VAT reports (L-5061). Done when the overlay reconciles with the reserve it assumes.

  5. Save or scan every receipt from the first day and reconcile on a fixed rhythm (L-3006). Ask vendors for invoices up front (L-3010). Done when no vendor invoice is missing a month after the event.

  6. Ask each funder for its lead time and file early (L-4039). As of 2026-11, the planning schedule names eight weeks before the event for Python Software Foundation and EuroPython funding, and recommends early June for a September event (S004). Done when each application is filed and booked as unconfirmed.

  7. Publish accounting patterns as sanitized templates with synthetic examples (L-5015). Done when a successor can run them without your bank data. See budget-sheet-template and cost-benchmarks.

Principles and general lessons#

[general]

lesson | scope: general | source: S023, S049, S098 | ledger: L-3007, L-4064, L-5008 | confidence: observed | status: active | basis: independent-conferences

Plan cash flow before you commit, because costs fall due before income does. The largest bills cluster in the final week, and a community that buys late leaves income thin until then.

Two single-conference statements stay below the promotion rule. Seattle held that a host should be paid fairly for its time and should not seek a large profit from the user community (L-1034). Maastricht treated accounting as a maintained operating instrument, with a plain-text budget and import rules (L-5013).

Risks#

Costs fall due before income arrives

risk | likelihood: medium | impact: high | mitigation: Name the bridge before signing and model the weekly balance through the final month | owner-role: treasurer | playbook: cash-flow-crunch

Judged for a mid-size conference. Several teams relied on loans or a personal card.

An unconfirmed grant is counted as committed income

risk | likelihood: medium | impact: high | mitigation: Book unconfirmed applications outside committed income until the funder confirms in writing | owner-role: treasurer

An unanswered request cannot fund a contract (L-5006).

A wrong VAT assumption changes the price

risk | likelihood: medium | impact: high | mitigation: Have a qualified adviser confirm the regime before prices are published | owner-role: treasurer

A wrong assumption surfaces as refund requests.

Late ticket sales are read as a revenue shortfall

risk | likelihood: medium | impact: medium | mitigation: Compare sales with a dated forecast before cutting commitments or raising prices | owner-role: treasurer | playbook: low-ticket-sales

Buyers purchase late.

A payout to a recipient in some countries fails

risk | likelihood: medium | impact: medium | mitigation: Check the payout route for each recipient country before promising reimbursement | owner-role: treasurer

The Foundation reimburses through a service that does not let charities send rupees to residents of India, and bank wires have also failed (L-4053).

A committed-spend overlay misstates totals

risk | likelihood: low | impact: medium | mitigation: Test the assumptions behind the overlay and retire it if other postings break the equivalence with its reserve | owner-role: treasurer

The overlay is report-only (L-5062).

Thin content#

Foundation share rate and form

thin | kind: stale | needs: A Board statement of the standing share of registration income and how it is collected | who-might-know: Foundation Board; Foundation Treasurer

Evidence from 2010 to 2023 shows 15, 20 and 10 percent, and no standing document states the rate for your year (R-010).

Budget and cash-flow requirements rest on hosting calls

thin | kind: stale | needs: A Board confirmation that a full budget and a cash-flow plan are still required in hosting proposals | who-might-know: Foundation Board; Conference Committee

R-004 and R-006 are documented only in hosting calls up to the 2020 call, so they carry no MUST badge.

University and Foundation VAT treatment

thin | kind: gap | needs: Verified rules per country on whether a university or the Foundation can sell tickets without VAT | who-might-know: Tax advisers in each host country; Foundation Board | area: jurisdiction-annexes

R-035 and R-036 are open questions, and the debrief reports an exemption that no source has verified.
