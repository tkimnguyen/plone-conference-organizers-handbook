Sustainability#

Why it matters#

A conference moves many people by air and train, fills bins and prints paper. The Plone Foundation board has adopted two priorities, more diversity in the community and a lower carbon footprint, and asks organizers to say when meeting them needs extra funding (L-4050; S005, as of 2026-11).

Past teams made pledges and small gestures, but no archived account measures a footprint or reports what worked. This chapter is therefore a short list of practical measures and an open invitation to contribute evidence.

Applies when#

  • all Every conference uses energy, creates waste and asks people to travel.

Decisions you must make#

  • OPTION Do you set a footprint goal and tell the Foundation what it needs? Register row R-028 (verified-current, as of 2026-11-02) records that organizers are encouraged to consider how to reduce the footprint. The Foundation asks to hear when a goal needs extra funding (L-4050). (decide-by: T-9m)

  • OPTION Which practical measures do you adopt? A call for proposals suggested recycling, avoiding disposables, minimal paper handouts, no low-quality swag and locally produced food (L-4071). A Bristol bid pledged to recycle waste and promote environmentally friendly travel (L-2059). (decide-by: T-6m)

  • OPTION Do you offer remote participation to reduce travel? One attendee suggested live broadcasting with remote questions to cut air travel (L-1129). (decide-by: T-6m)

  • OPTION What happens to equipment and material after the event? Bristol donated the wireless uplink to a local non-profit (L-2034). (decide-by: T-1m)

How-to#

  1. Ask the venue and the caterer what they recycle and whether they can avoid disposables. Done: a written answer for each.

  2. Keep printed handouts minimal and choose swag for quality, as described in Print and merch (L-4071; S049). Done: the print and swag list shows what was left out.

  3. Prefer locally produced food in the catering brief (L-4071; S049). Done: the brief names the preference.

  4. Decide on remote access, using Hybrid and online. Done: a recorded decision.

  5. Plan what happens to equipment after the event, for example a donation (L-2034; S016). Done: a list with a receiver per item.

  6. Tell the Foundation if a goal needs extra funding (L-4050; S005). Done: a dated message to the board.
