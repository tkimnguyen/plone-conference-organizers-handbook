Workstreams#

The workstreams are the knowledge base: thirty chapters, each with decisions to make, a how-to, general and specific lessons, risks, and honest thin markers. Pick the chapter that matches the task in front of you, or read a whole group.

Money and law#

  • Finance — budget, cash flow, VAT regimes, grants and the hledger setup.

  • Sponsors — tiers, prospectus, sponsor care and down-market reality.

  • Ticketing and registration — pricing, platforms, comps, global south tickets and late buying.

  • Contracts, legal and insurance — venue contracts, cancellation clauses and insurance due diligence.

  • Privacy and GDPR — consent, lists, sponsor data and handover of personal data.

Programme#

  • Programme portfolio — trainings, conference days, side tracks and sprints as separate products.

  • CFP and selection — the call, the criteria, the committee and the timeline.

  • Scheduling — building the agenda, breaks, the Foundation meeting slot and the freeze.

  • Keynotes and invited talks — finding and announcing headline speakers.

  • Speakers and speaker care — communication, materials, introductions and feedback.

  • Trainings — trainer terms, the quality floor and registration.

  • Business and side tracks — running a track for an audience beyond the community.

  • Sprints — purpose, venues, newcomer teams and coordination.

People#

  • Volunteers and roles — staffing, feeding, equipping and thanking the crew.

  • Code of Conduct operations — publication, agreement and incident response.

  • Accessibility and inclusion — physical access, legibility, language and financial help.

  • Health, safety and emergencies — a thin chapter with honest gaps.

  • Visas and travel — invitation letters, timing and travel guidance.

Marketing#

  • Marketing and outreach — the permission-first method, local activation and measurement.

  • Web, comms and brand — the website, the QA gate, accounts and the brand.

Venue and logistics#

  • Venue — capacity, layout, contracts and the hotel-integrated trade-off.

  • Catering — food strategy, queues, dietary needs and water.

  • Hotels and local logistics — room blocks, distances and local transport.

  • City selection — what makes a city work for this conference.

On-site production#

  • AV, recording and internet — Wi-Fi capacity, recording as a workstream, PeerTube and streaming.

  • Print and merch — shirts, badges, schedules and buffers.

  • Signage and showtime — the registration desk, signage and day-one setup.

Social#

  • Social events and party — the party, engineered evenings and the alcohol rules.

Formats and values#

  • Hybrid and online — the streaming trade-off and the online editions.

  • Sustainability — what little is known, marked thin.
