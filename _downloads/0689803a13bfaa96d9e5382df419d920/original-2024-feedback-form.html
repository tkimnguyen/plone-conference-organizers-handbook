Plone Conference Feedback

Thank you so much for participating in Plone Conference / PythonCerrado 2024!

Please tell us what you liked about the Plone Conference 2024 and what could be improved. Rate the sessions from 1-5 (5 is the best) and give your suggestions and overall feedback in writing.

Your feedback is valuable for making the future events even more enjoyable. Thank you!

PS. If you want to your feedback answered, please input your email address. Otherwise, you may fill in the form anonymously.

Feedback

Please fill this short survey and help us improve the event in the future.

What parts of the conference did you attend? (mark all that apply)
Training: Rate the overall quality and relevance of the training you attended? (1 - Poor, 5 - Excellent)
Keynotes and talks: Rate the overall quality and relevance of the keynotes and talks (1 - Poor, 5 - Excellent)
Conference organizing: How well-organized was the conference in terms of registration, communication, scheduling, venue arrangements, and overall logistics? (1 - Poor, 5 - Excellent)
Food and catering: How would you rate your overall experience with the catering and food services provided during the conference? (1 - Poor, 5 - Excellent)
Overall score: How would you rate your overall feelings about the conference? (1 - Poor, 5 - Excellent)

Please add your email if you want a response to your feedback.

Thank you!
