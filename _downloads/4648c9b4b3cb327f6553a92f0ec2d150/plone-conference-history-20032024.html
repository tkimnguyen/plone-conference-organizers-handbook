                                                              Plone Conferences

                                                              The Plone Conference, the annual gathering of the vibrant Plone community, has been a cornerstone of the open-source CMS world for nearly two decades. From the first edition, back in 2003 to its latest edition in 2024, the conference has evolved into a global celebration of collaboration, innovation, and knowledge sharing.

                                                              Take a look at the previous conferences throughout the years.

                                                              2024 - Brasília, Brazil

                                                              The 2024 Plone Conference took place in Brasília, Brazil, from November 26 to December 1, 2024, with 291 attendees and 80 talks. The Conference was held together with a Brazilian regional Python Conference: Python Cerrado.

                                                              https://2024.ploneconf.org/

                                                              2023 - Eibar, Basque Country

                                                              The 2023 Plone Conference took place in Eibar, Basque Country, from October 2 to 8, 2023, with 162 attendees, and more than 1000 unique viewers in the online transmission.

                                                              https://2023.ploneconf.org/

                                                              2022 - Namur, Belgium

                                                              Namur, Belgium, welcomed 151 attendees to the Plone community from October 10 to 16, 2022. The conference also had more than 1400 unique viewers in its online transmission.

                                                              https://2022.ploneconf.org/

                                                              2021 - Online

                                                              Due to the ongoing Covid-19 pandemic, the 2021 edition of the Plone Conference was held online, from October 23 to 31, with 268 attendees.

                                                              2020 - Online

                                                              In response to the Covid-19 pandemic, the Plone Conference transitioned to an online format in 2020, with 295 attendees participating during December 5-13.

                                                              2019 - Ferrara, Italy

                                                              Ferrara, Italy, was the setting for the 2019 Plone Conference from October 21 to 27, with 173 attendees.

                                                              2018 - Tokyo, Japan

                                                              The Plone Conference ventured to Asia, for the first time in 2019. The megacity of Tokyo, Japan, was the setting for the Conference, which took place from November 5 to 11, 2018, with around 150 attendees.

                                                              2017 - Barcelona, Catalonia

                                                              Barcelona, Catalonia, welcomed 173 attendees to the Plone community from October 16 to 22, 2017, providing a sunny backdrop for discussions and networking.

                                                              2016 - Boston, United States

                                                              Boston, United States, played host to the Plone Conference in 2016, from October 17 to 23, with 150 attendees, offering a warm welcome back to the U.S. East Coast.

                                                              2015 - Bucharest, Romania

                                                              Bucharest, Romania, became the center of Plone activities from October 12 to 18, 2015, allowing the 200 attendees to explore the wonders of Eastern Europe.

                                                              2014 - Bristol, United Kingdom

                                                              Bristol, United Kingdom, once again hosted the Plone Conference in 2014. This edition took place from October 27 to 31, with 150 attendees.

                                                              2013 - Brasilia, Brazil

                                                              In 2013, the Plone community was welcomed to South America by the Brazilian Python community. With 584 attendees, this edition was held in Brasilia, Brazil, from September 30 to October 6.

                                                              2012 - Arnhem, Netherlands

                                                              Arnhem, Netherlands, welcomed 250 attendees to the Plone community from October 8 to 14, 2012, offering a taste of Dutch culture along with a diverse range of Plone-related discussions.

                                                              2011 - San Francisco, United States

                                                              San Francisco, United States, provided the backdrop for the 2011 Plone Conference from November 1 to 8, with 265 attendees, that enjoyed the laid-back West Coast vibe while diving deep into Plone topics.

                                                              2010 - Bristol, United Kingdom

                                                              Bristol, United Kingdom, played host to the Plone Conference in 2010, from October 25–31, with 275 attendees.

                                                              2009 - Budapest, Hungary

                                                              Budapest, Hungary, took center stage from October 28 to 30, 2009, with 420 attendees.

                                                              2008 - Washington DC, United States

                                                              The nation's capital, Washington DC, hosted the Plone Conference in 2008, from October 6 to 12. This event brought together 350 Plone enthusiasts amidst the backdrop of historic landmarks.

                                                              2007 - Naples, Italy

                                                              Naples, Italy, welcomed both a rich historical backdrop and over 400 passionate Plone enthusiasts who gathered from October 8 to 12, 2007, to savor incredible food and dive into the world of Plone.

                                                              2006 - Seattle, United States

                                                              In 2006, 350 Plonistas ventured to Seattle, United States, from October 25 to 27, for the fourth Plone Conference. The Pacific Northwest provided a unique backdrop for collaboration and networking.

                                                              2005 - Vienna, Austria

                                                              The Plone Conference returned to Vienna, Austria, in 2005, further solidifying its European presence. Attendees enjoyed the city's charm and explored the latest developments in the Plone ecosystem.

                                                              2004 - Vienna, Austria

                                                              Vienna, Austria, played host to the Plone Conference in 2004, bringing the conference to the heart of Europe. Attendees reveled in the rich history of this picturesque city while diving deep into Plone's potential.

                                                              2003 - New Orleans, United States

                                                              The inaugural Plone Conference took place in the heart of New Orleans, United States, from October 15 to 17, 2003. With this edition, Plone enthusiasts from around the world united to kickstart a tradition that would continue for years to come.
