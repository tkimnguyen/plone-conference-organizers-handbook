                                    Own your digital future

                                    For one week, the Plone community gathers in Maastricht to build and govern trustworthy digital platforms focussing on digital autonomy, AI, and safer decisions in uncertain times.

                                    Plone Conference 2026 is over!

                                    Thank you all for joining us in Maastricht!

                                    From 21 to 27 September, the Plone Community spent a week in Maastricht: two days of trainings, three days of talks and keynotes, a Thursday night at Muziekgieterij that ran past midnight, and a weekend of sprints to close. A new full-day Business Track ran in parallel on Friday — Digital Autonomy &amp; AI — where the president of the FSFE and seven practitioners from agencies, public administrations, and the Plone community argued that open source is the practical route to digital sovereignty, and the conversation carried straight into the sprint room. None of it happens without the speakers, trainers, sprinters, sponsors, volunteers, and the crew behind the scenes: thank you. The conference is over; the work continues.See you in Bucharest in 2027.

                                    Why This Conference Matters Now

                                    Organizations across Europe and beyond are reassessing their digital foundations.

                                    • “How do we keep control of our platforms and data?”
                                    • “How do we adopt AI responsibly — with governance and accountability?”
                                    • “How do we reduce vendor lock‑in and keep exit options?”
                                    • “How do we meet accessibility, security, and compliance requirements?”

                                    Plone Conference 2026 addresses these questions through real systems, real governance models, and real-world deployments.

                                    This is not a theoretical discussion. It is a working meeting of professionals building independent, trustworthy digital infrastructure.

                                    Friday 25 September · Business Track

                                    Digital Autonomy & AI

                                    The Business Track — Digital Autonomy & AI — is one day inside the conference, built for IT decision makers and digital leaders: in English, without requiring prior Plone knowledge. An independent keynote opens the day, and seven senior practitioners carry one argument in a deliberate arc.

                                    What is the Plone Conference?

                                    Plone Conference is the annual global event of the Plone Foundation — the non-profit steward of the Plone open-source content management system.

                                    Each year, developers, agencies, public institutions, and decision makers meet to:

                                    • Share technical expertise

                                    • Present production deployments

                                    • Strengthen the ecosystem

                                    • Shape the roadmap

                                    • Contribute directly to the software

                                    It combines technical depth with strategic clarity — so teams can make decisions they can defend.

                                    Learn

                                    Gain direct insight into Plone and Volto from the people building and running it in production.

                                    • Frontend and backend architecture

                                    • Accessibility and compliance

                                    • Security and governance

                                    • AI integration in open systems

                                    • Sustainable platform operations

                                    Connect

                                    Meet the people behind the code, the deployments, and the decisions.

                                    • Core contributors

                                    • Service providers

                                    • Public institutions

                                    • Enterprise users

                                    • Open-source leaders

                                    The relationships built here extend far beyond the conference week.

                                    Advance

                                    Return with clarity.

                                    • Better architectural decisions

                                    • Stronger governance models

                                    • Confidence around AI adoption

                                    • A roadmap aligned with digital autonomy

                                    Who Should Attend?

                                    Plone Conference 2026 brings together professionals building or evaluating independent digital platforms — with control over data, dependencies, and long-term outcomes.

                                    Service Providers & Agencies

                                    The Location — Maastricht, Europe

                                    Maastricht sits at the crossroads of the Netherlands, Belgium, and Germany — a natural meeting point for an international open-source community: well connected, compact, and welcoming. It offers the right setting for a week of focused sessions, shared meals, and the conversations that continue after the talks end.

                                    The conference takes place at Van der Valk Maastricht, offering modern conference facilities and integrated accommodation.

                                    The city combines:

                                    • Strong European identity
                                    • Easy international accessibility
                                    • A compact historic center
                                    • High-quality hospitality
