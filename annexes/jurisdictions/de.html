Annex: Germany#

This annex is general information, not legal, tax or insurance advice. Check current rules with a qualified local adviser.

What this annex is for#

This annex looks at the other side of the 2026 cross-border VAT experience: the organizer whose tax home is in Germany. It is a stub. No German conference account is in the archive, so everything here comes from an event held abroad by a German-tax company. It records an experience and states no tax rule. Jurisdiction-specific VAT guidance needs a tax professional, and nobody has written it for this handbook.

Applies when#

  • jurisdiction=DE The organizing entity has its tax home in Germany, or the conference takes place in Germany.

What organizers reported#

An organizer with a German tax home#

The 2026 conference took place in the Netherlands and was organized by a company with its tax home in Germany. The organizers reported that the mismatch between the organizer's country and the event's country made VAT a top-level challenge (L-5001, S098). The full case note is under finance#case-notes, and the Dutch side is in the Netherlands annex.

Decide the regime and the seller first#

The organizers advise resolving the applicable VAT regime before you publish ticket prices (L-5000, S098). They also advise comparing ticket-selling entities by the final price paid by participants who cannot recover VAT (L-5002, S098). Which regime applies to a German-based organizer selling tickets for an event abroad is not recorded here. The decision framing is in finance.

Risks#

  • The organizer assumes its home-country tax treatment applies to an event abroad. Likelihood medium, impact high. Ask an adviser who knows both countries before pricing.

  • German-side reporting duties for foreign ticket income are missed. Likelihood medium, impact medium. Put the question on the adviser's list at T-12m.

  • A German conference has no archived precedent for its legal form or venue contract. Likelihood high, impact low. Treat the first German organizer as a documentation opportunity and record decisions.
